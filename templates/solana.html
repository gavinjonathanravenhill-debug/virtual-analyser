Checks every wallet every few minutes using Solana's free public RPC, so it builds up history from when the server started (last ~15 transactions per wallet on the first pass). BUY/SELL = token swapped against SOL/USDC/USDT. IN/OUT = plain transfer. Price is what they actually paid.
This is a test bench, not trade calls. Every key-wallet move ≥ $5k (and every Vine trade ≥ $250) is logged with the price when we saw it, then re-priced after 1h, 24h and 7d. Return is direction-adjusted: positive means price went the way their trade pointed (up after a buy, down after a sell). After a few weeks this shows which wallets actually lead price. Paper-trade it before risking money; not financial advice.
