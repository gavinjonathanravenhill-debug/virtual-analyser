Robinhood keeps a small float in its hot wallet and sweeps the excess into storage. Steady sweeps into storage = customers holding more of that coin overall.
This is a test bench, not trade calls. Every key-wallet move ≥ $5k is logged with the price when we saw it, then re-priced after 1h, 24h and 7d. Return is direction-adjusted: positive means price went the way their trade pointed (up after a buy, down after a sell). After a few weeks this shows which wallets actually lead price.
